# 📚 PorBhai

### Your AI-powered learning companion for students

PorBhai is an educational platform designed to help students from **Class 1 to Class 12** learn, practice, and understand their study materials in a simple and interactive way.

The goal of PorBhai is to bring **study materials + an AI teacher + practice tools** into one platform.

---

## 🎯 Vision

Learning should not be limited to a classroom or a textbook.

PorBhai aims to give students a place where they can:

* 📖 Find study materials
* 🧠 Understand difficult topics
* 🤖 Ask an AI teacher questions
* ✍️ Practice with quizzes
* 📊 Track their learning progress
* 🌐 Learn in Bangla and English

---

## 🚀 Planned Features

### 📚 Study Materials

Study materials organized by:

* Class 1–12
* Subject
* Chapter
* Topic

Possible resources include:

* Notes
* Chapter summaries
* Practice questions
* MCQs
* Educational resources

---

### 🤖 PorBhai AI

An AI-powered study assistant that can explain lessons like a teacher.

Students will be able to ask questions such as:

> "Explain photosynthesis in simple Bangla."

or

> "I don't understand this math problem. Can you explain it step by step?"

The AI should focus on **helping students understand concepts**, rather than simply giving answers.

---

### 📝 Quizzes & Practice

Students will be able to practice what they learn through:

* MCQs
* Chapter quizzes
* Practice questions
* Topic-based tests

---

### 📊 Student Dashboard

Students will eventually be able to:

* Track learning progress
* Save materials
* Continue previous lessons
* View quiz results
* Track completed chapters

## 💡 Why PorBhai?

Students often have access to books and online resources but may still struggle to understand difficult topics.

PorBhai aims to make learning more accessible by combining educational content with an interactive AI tutor.
