Welcome to PorBhai

PorBhai is a learning website made for students from Class 1 to Class 12. Its purpose is simple: to give every student one clear place to find the study materials they need. Instead of searching through many websites, books, and messages, a student can choose their class from the menu at the top of the page and start studying right away. Every class has its own page, so students see only the materials meant for them, and they can return at any time to revise. PorBhai is made for everyone: young learners who are just beginning school, older students preparing for important exams, parents who want to know what their children should study, and teachers who need one reliable place to send their students for revision. Whatever your class and whatever your goal, PorBhai is here to help you take the next step. Studying well begins with having the right material at the right time. Many students lose valuable hours simply looking for what to read, and when materials are scattered, it is easy to feel confused, to miss an important topic, or to give up before even starting. PorBhai tackles this problem by keeping everything organised class by class, so students can spend their energy on learning instead of searching. This matters because not every student has the same access to books, notes, or guidance at home. A website that gathers study materials in a simple and clear way can help more students keep up with their lessons and feel more confident in class. Learning should never depend on how much help a student can find alone, and PorBhai is built with that belief. Every student deserves a fair chance to learn, and a well-organised website is a small but real step toward that. Good study habits also start early. When a student can open a page and know exactly what to read, studying becomes a routine instead of a struggle, and over time that routine builds focus, discipline, and the confidence to ask questions and aim higher. Our goal is to make studying feel clear, organised, and less stressful. A student who knows where to find their materials can plan their time better, revise before tests with confidence, and build habits that last for many years. Studying is not only about reading more; it is about reading the right things in the right order, and a clear structure helps students do exactly that. PorBhai will keep growing, with new materials added over time, because learning never stops and neither should the resources that support it. The website is designed, built, and maintained by Anni, a full stack programmer and software engineer intern, with one simple idea in mind: technology should make learning easier for every student. Ready to begin? Choose your class from the menu at the top and start learning today. We are happy to have you here.
